    Check your own documents as a deal

    Choose a folder from your computer (its subfolders are kept) or a few files. They become a new deal here: every document is checked, problems are marked on the pages, and you can export and download the marked copies.

    PDF (including scans), Word, text, CSV and HTML; up to 15 MB each and 50 per deal.

    Deals you add here are listed only in this browser, and you can remove them. They are stored on the demo server until then, so use practice documents, not confidential ones.

    How it works

    1. Put the documents in a folder

      One folder per deal, inside your deals folder. Subfolders are fine.

    2. Open it here

      Every document is checked. Each problem is marked in the text, with what is wrong and what to ask.

    3. Remove false marks, then export

      Not a problem? Remove the mark. Export writes marked copies into the folder, ready to send.

    Or just ask

    Press Ask at the bottom right and speak or type: open Acme, what's wrong with the customer contract, next problem, that's not a problem, export.
